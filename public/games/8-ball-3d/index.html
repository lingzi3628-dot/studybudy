HOW TO PLAY
Quick guide

🎯 Aim (pull-back-and-release): Click + drag from the cue ball in the OPPOSITE direction of where you want it to go. Like pulling a slingshot back — release to launch the ball forward.

⚡ Power: Drag distance = power. Drag farther for harder shots.

⚪ Guidelines: White line = cue ball path. Yellow line = where the target ball will go after impact.

🎱 Goal: Sink your group (solids 1-7 OR stripes 9-15) before the AI sinks theirs. Then sink the 8-ball to win!

📸 Camera: Right-click + drag to orbit. Scroll wheel to zoom.

🪙 Coins: Earn 50 coins per win + 5 per ball sunk. Spend in the shop on premium skins.
